    A LITTLE LESS TO REMEMBER

    Two places.
    One way back.

    PartCue keeps a manual memory of where each part came from and where you put it. Start with the interactive enclosure, or explore a recorded example.

    1. Record Choose a removed part and a free tray. Add its quantity and a note.

    2. Return Follow the sequence in reverse. Amber marks the pickup. Cyan marks the saved origin.

    3. Confirm Select the next part, put it back, and confirm the exact quantity. Pause or undo whenever needed.

    4. Keep Save privately to your account, or export a JSON backup. Import gives each copy a new identity.

    This browser workbench is a simulation with four predefined parts. Your marks and confirmations drive the sequence. It does not recognize real objects or verify physical assembly. The separate SPECS Lens runs in Lens Studio; browser saves do not restore Lens anchors.
    YOUR PRIVATE WORKSPACE

    Your account.

    Privacy and your data

    Session titles, notes, quantities and positions are stored privately in Firebase under your account. Save is explicit. Export JSON for your own backup. This browser tab keeps a temporary recovery copy until you sign out or close the tab. No analytics or advertising trackers are included.

    Up to 50 saved sessions per account. Avoid putting sensitive information in your notes.

    Delete my account and saved sessions

    This permanently removes your cloud sessions and account. Export any work you need first. Enter your current password to confirm ownership.
